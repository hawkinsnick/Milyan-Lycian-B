# Critical source acquisition checklist

1. Resolve complete bibliographic identities for Schürr 2005 (TL 55), 2016 (TL 44c), and 2018 (TL 44d); currently discovery leads only.
2. Find lawful library access or author/publisher open copies and document URL, access date, page and plate references.
3. Confirm whether copying a transcription is licensed; when not, retain only independently authored metadata and bibliographic locators.
4. Collate source readings against TITUS and record disagreements without choosing silently.
5. Where images are lawful, note photographic provenance, inscription orientation, physical damage and reading confidence.
6. Have an independent reviewer examine disputed readings before marking verified.

Never bypass paywalls or redistribute scanned books without permission.
