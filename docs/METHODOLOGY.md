# Editorial methodology

1. Register an inscription only after checking a traceable edition, photograph, squeeze, or reliable scholarly catalog.
2. Preserve the edition's identifier, source bibliographic citation, and precise locator (page, plate, inscription number).
3. Maintain separate diplomatic and normalized readings; preserve line boundaries and original signs where supported.
4. Encode uncertainty and restoration without silently completing damaged text.
5. Track alternative readings as attributed scholarly claims, not merged consensus.
6. Record provenance, date range and basis, object type, writing direction, and rights for any image or transcription.
7. Require independent review before promoting provisional records to verified status.
8. Document coverage gaps, negative findings, and validation outcomes.

Initial priorities: identify authoritative editions of the Xanthos bilingual/trilingual monument's Milyan material and other securely attributed Milyan inscriptions; verify edition identifiers before data entry. Avoid importing material from Lycian A merely because it shares a script family.
