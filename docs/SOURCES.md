# Bibliographic source register

- TITUS, *Corpus of Lycian and Milyan (Lycian B) Inscriptions*, based on Kalinka, Friedrich, Neumann, Metzger and other editions; reedited by H. C. Melchert. https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/anatol/lycian/lyccot.htm. **Rights:** check before bulk transcription reuse.
- Martínez Rodríguez, *Lycian hba-. Towards the interpretation of TL 44a.49*. Identifies TL 44 Milyan c32–65 and d1–71. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369020329_Lycian_hba-_Towards_the_interpretation_of_TL_44a49.
- Facella, *Xanthus Stele's Epigram* (2018), DOI 10.30687/Axon/2532-6848/2018/01/007. Context and proposed dating, not an edition of Milyan lines.
- Yakubovich, 2008 dissertation, University of Chicago, discussion of two attested Milyan inscription complexes: https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/yakubovich_diss_2008.pdf.
