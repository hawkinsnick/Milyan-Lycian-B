# Scholarly quality model

## Separate readiness dimensions
1. **Coverage:** known witness inventory, gaps and competing identifications.
2. **Fidelity:** edition-level readings, lines, lacunae, restoration marks and alternative readings.
3. **Provenance:** bibliographic locator for each editorial claim, independent-source assessment.
4. **Rights:** reproduction rights for transcriptions, images and drawings.
5. **Interoperability:** stable IDs, export formats, native-unit semantics and loss reporting.
6. **Reproducibility:** validation, CI, pinned data versions, deterministic outputs.
7. **Research usability:** concordance, KWIC, searchable line contexts, critical apparatus and scholar-friendly documentation.
8. **AI integrity:** corpus skill and master registry synced with actual verified data; no synthetic translations.

A high score in infrastructure cannot compensate for zero verified readings. Keep status multidimensional rather than inventing an overall grade.
