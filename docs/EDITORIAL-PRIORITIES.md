# Editorial priorities and current source audit

A modern dissertation's index identifies Schürr (2016) for TL 44c 32ff., Schürr (2018) for TL 44d, and Schürr (2005) for TL 55 as relevant editions. This significantly improves the edition-reconciliation roadmap, but does **not** constitute collation of the underlying editions.

Primary discovery source: https://diposit.ub.edu/dspace/bitstream/2445/182952/4/EMR_PhD_THESIS.pdf (section 5.2, index of Lycian inscriptions).

Cross-check with Melchert's TITUS corpus: https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/anatol/lycian/lyccot.htm. TITUS itself warns that its editorial readings must be checked. Record text rights and publication-specific attribution before importing any substantial transcription.

For each edition, build a diplomatic line map and a normalized transliteration layer with independently attributed variants, sign uncertainty and restorations. Keep physical monument and textual segment counts separate. Until collation, all records remain provisional.
